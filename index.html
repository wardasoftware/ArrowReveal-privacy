Privacy Policy for ArrowReveal

Effective Date: October 24, 2026

Developer: WardaSoftware

WardaSoftware ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how ArrowReveal ("the App") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

ArrowReveal is a mobile puzzle game. Some gameplay information is stored locally on your device, while third-party services may process information to provide advertising, analytics, crash reporting, in-app purchases, gaming features, and push notifications.

This policy describes our intended data practices. The precise information processed depends on the features you use and the configuration of the App and its third-party SDKs.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Local Gameplay and Settings Data

ArrowReveal stores certain gameplay information and preferences locally on your device, which may include:

  • Puzzle progression and completed levels.
  • Earned stars, best moves, and completion times.
  • Unlocked gallery artwork and locally maintained achievements.
  • Sound, vibration, theme, and other gameplay preferences.
  • Purchase entitlement status used by the App, where applicable.

This information is stored using Android storage mechanisms, such as SharedPreferences, where applicable. We do not operate our own backend to store this local gameplay information.

Local gameplay data is not intentionally transmitted to our own servers. However, gameplay events, diagnostic information, or related identifiers may be processed by third-party analytics, gaming, advertising, and crash-reporting services as described below.

Local data may be lost if you clear the App's storage, uninstall the App, or use an available reset feature. Deleting local data does not automatically delete information held by third parties.

1.2 Advertising — Google AdMob

ArrowReveal uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Depending on the release and enabled features, these may include banner, interstitial, rewarded, and app-open advertisements.

AdMob and associated advertising services may process:

  • Advertising identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID where available and permitted.
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it.
  • Device model, operating system, language, and app version.
  • Ad impressions, clicks, interactions, and measurement information.
  • Technical diagnostics and information used to detect invalid advertising activity.

This information may be used to deliver and measure advertisements, limit repetitive ads, prevent fraud, and provide personalized or non-personalized advertising, depending on applicable law, consent, and SDK configuration.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

1.3 Consent Management and Privacy Choices

Where Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) or another consent mechanism is enabled, it may be used to present advertising consent choices and manage privacy preferences where required.

Available choices depend on your location, applicable requirements, and the consent-management functionality enabled in the App. Consent choices may affect how advertising information is processed.

1.4 Analytics — Firebase Analytics and Mixpanel

ArrowReveal uses Firebase Analytics and Mixpanel to understand how players use the game and to improve gameplay, usability, and performance.

Depending on the SDK configuration and events enabled, these services may process:

  • Gameplay events, such as level starts, level completions, and feature interactions.
  • App usage, session, and engagement information.
  • App instance identifiers and other pseudonymous identifiers.
  • Device, operating system, and app version information.
  • Event timestamps and technical usage information.

We use analytics information to understand gameplay patterns, evaluate features, improve level design, and identify areas where the App can be improved.

We do not intentionally send names, email addresses, or other directly identifying information to analytics services unless separately disclosed and appropriately configured.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Mixpanel Privacy Policy: https://mixpanel.com/legal/privacy-policy/

1.5 Crash Reporting — Firebase Crashlytics

ArrowReveal uses Firebase Crashlytics to identify and investigate crashes and technical errors.

Depending on the configuration, Crashlytics may process:

  • Crash reports, stack traces, and exception details.
  • Device model, operating system version, and app version.
  • App state and diagnostic information associated with an error.
  • Timestamps, app instance identifiers, and relevant technical logs.

We use this information to troubleshoot errors, improve stability, and maintain the App.

Firebase Privacy Information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

1.6 In-App Purchases — Google Play Billing

ArrowReveal uses Google Play Billing to process in-app purchases, including the Remove Ads purchase where offered.

Google Play handles payment transactions. We do not directly collect or store your complete credit card number, bank account credentials, or other full payment credentials through ArrowReveal.

Google Play Billing may process purchase information, transaction identifiers, product information, and purchase status to complete transactions and verify entitlements.

The App may store purchase entitlement status locally and query Google Play Billing to verify whether a purchase is valid. Prices and purchase terms are displayed through Google Play at the time of purchase.

Google Play Terms: https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/about/play-terms/

1.7 Google Play Games

ArrowReveal integrates Google Play Games services for supported gaming features, which may include achievements, leaderboards, and saved-game synchronization where implemented and enabled.

When you use these features, Google Play Games may process information associated with your gaming profile, achievements, leaderboard submissions, and supported saved-game data. Information processed depends on the features you use and your sign-in choices.

Local gameplay data is separate from information synchronized through Google Play Games. Cloud synchronization is available only for supported features that are actually enabled.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

1.8 Push Notifications — Firebase Cloud Messaging

ArrowReveal uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to support push notifications where this functionality is enabled.

FCM may process information such as device registration tokens, app and device information, and message delivery information necessary to support notifications.

Notifications may be used for supported game updates, reminders, or other app-related messages, depending on the features enabled in the current release.

You can manage notification permissions through your Android device settings. Disabling notifications may prevent you from receiving certain messages.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

2. How We Use Information

Information processed through ArrowReveal and its integrated services may be used to:

  • Provide puzzle gameplay, progression, and supported gallery features.
  • Save gameplay settings and progress locally.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Process and verify in-app purchases.
  • Provide supported Google Play Games features.
  • Deliver supported push notifications.
  • Understand feature usage and improve game design.
  • Detect, investigate, and resolve crashes and technical issues.
  • Maintain app security and prevent abuse.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

ArrowReveal integrates third-party services that may receive or process information as necessary to provide their functionality.

  • Google AdMob: Advertising delivery, measurement, and related services.
  • Google UMP: Consent management and privacy choices where enabled.
  • Firebase Analytics: App analytics and usage measurement.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Crash reporting and diagnostics.

  • Google Play Games: Supported gaming features, achievements, leaderboards, and cloud saves where enabled.

These providers process information under their applicable terms, privacy policies, and data-processing practices. They may use their own service providers or subprocessors.

We do not sell your personal information as a standalone product. Advertising and analytics providers may process information for their own service purposes as described in their policies and subject to applicable law and your privacy choices.

We do not operate our own backend for the local gameplay data described in Section 1.1.

A reset option, if available, affects only the local information covered by that option and does not necessarily delete information held by third-party services.

Third-Party Data

Information collected by Google, Firebase, Mixpanel, Google Play Billing, and Google Play Games is subject to the applicable provider's retention, deletion, and account-management policies. We do not control all third-party retention periods.

Privacy Requests

For privacy-related questions or requests concerning information processed through the App, contact wardasoftware@gmail.com. We will assess requests and respond in accordance with applicable law and the capabilities available to us.

Purchases and Cloud-Saved Data

Purchase records may need to remain available to Google Play for transaction, accounting, security, or legal purposes.

If supported Google Play Games data is synchronized to your account, you may be able to manage or delete relevant game data through Google Play Games or your Google Account settings, subject to Google's available controls and policies.

Deleting local App data does not automatically delete purchase records, analytics records, crash reports, or cloud-saved information held by third parties.
